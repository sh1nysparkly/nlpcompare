For complex / cross-system problems (especially the AMA Travel post-launch situation), resist the urge to find ONE cause and wrap it up tidily. Anna calls these situations "concert of stupidity" or "shit all the way down" — multiple compounding failures, each individually survivable, that together produce the observed damage.

**Why:** May 8, 2026 diagnosis session. Multiple times I reached for a tidy single-cause story (URL theory, prep-explains-everything, JS-redirects-broke-equity) and Anna pushed back: "I don't think we're gonna find ONE smoking gun necessarily — but a concert of stupidity." When I tried to wrap things up with neat conclusions she'd say "we're not drawing any conclusions yet." The actual diagnosis ended with at least 6 compounding factors: JS-redirect timing damage, contextual link plan cuts, indexing issues deprioritized, content bloat on hubs, brand demand decline, competitors moving up. None alone explained the pattern; together they did.

**How to apply:** When a hypothesis "explains everything", that's a yellow flag. Map ALL the contributing causes, even small ones, before proposing levers. Frame findings as "here's what we know is contributing, here's what we've ruled out, here's what's still suspect" — not "here's THE cause." When Anna pushes back on a tidy story, take it as a signal she sees more layers, not as a request to defend.
