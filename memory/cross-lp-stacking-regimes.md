When stacking two within-recipe levers (e.g., P3b combo permutations), the compound effect on NLP measurements falls into one of three regimes. Source: Bean synthesis from Phase C Tier-2 measurements across Hotels, Cruises, and VP (May 12 2026), validated against Anna's empirical Lab measurements.

**Three regimes:**

1. **Same-category-layer (or same-metric) levers → multiplicative compound.** Both levers touch the same measurement (e.g., brand-Σ). Combined effect exceeds linear sum of individual contributions. **Hotels P3b** (P2a Partners-first-in-Hero + P3a Articles-ghosted): both levers touched brand-Σ via different mechanisms (position-lift + entity-space-freeing). Brand-Σ 4.2× × 1.6× → 6.2× baseline, exceeded Phase B T9's 0.029.

2. **Different-layer levers → linear-additive composition.** One lever moves category-layer, another moves entity-layer; predictions matched empirical results to 3 decimals. **Cruises P3b** (P2b FR-FAQs-first entity-layer + P2c TPC-consolidated category-layer): top-cat 0.9020, VO 0.3053, "Guide" entity 0.3199, all = sum of individual contributions.

3. **Same-entity-space levers → modest under-additivity.** Both levers redistribute salience within the same entity neighborhood, partially overlapping. Combined effect ≈ 60-80% of pure linear sum. **VP P3b** (P2a Value-Props-first within-Hero + P2b FR-FAQs-before-Articles): both interventions touched Mexico-adjacent salience. Mexico entity lift +0.0116 vs predicted +0.020.

**Why:** Stacking shape depends on whether the levers operate on independent layers/metrics or share an entity-space. Same-metric levers with different mechanisms compound. Different-layer levers don't interfere. Same-entity-space levers partially cannibalize each other's salience-redistribution.

**Why: it's a methodology principle for Phase D planning and future LP work.** Classify each lever by which NLP-measurement layer (and which entity space, if entity-layer) it touches BEFORE predicting compound outcomes. Don't assume linear additivity universally; check the lever-overlap structure first.

**How to apply:** Before designing or recommending a stacked combo on any LP, ask: (a) which layers do the two levers touch? (b) if same layer, which metric/entity-space? (c) is the expected compound multiplicative, linear-additive, or under-additive? Then size expectations accordingly. For Phase D / optimization-guide framing, lever combos that fall in regime 1 (multiplicative) are the highest-leverage push; regime 2 (linear-additive) is the most predictable; regime 3 (under-additive) is worth measuring rather than estimating.

**Confirmed n:** 3 LPs (Hotels P3b, Cruises P3b, VP P3b) as of May 12 2026. Pattern is structural per the two-layer NLP measurement model; expected to generalize but cross-LP n is small.
