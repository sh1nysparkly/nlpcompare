A page losing keyword rankings is NOT the same as a page failing. Before describing a page as "tanking" or "winning", layer multiple signals: rank/visibility (Ahrefs), clicks/impressions (GSC), conversion or sales data (her internal stack), and Anna's qualitative read of the situation. Each can tell a different story; together they reveal whether the page is genuinely broken or just shifting its traffic mix.

**Why:** May 8, 2026 session. I built an audit on Ahrefs keyword position changes alone and concluded a half-dozen pages were "tanking" because they'd lost head-term rankings. Anna corrected: car-rentals had clicks "exploded" and was a top-selling product, hotels had improved overall clicks, travel-insurance overall had improved. Meanwhile VP and Cruises were genuinely tanking on rank AND clicks AND sales. The right diagnosis required separating those classes of "loss". She also flagged that "ama travel" search VOLUME itself was declining — context I couldn't have gotten from APIs alone.

**How to apply:** Before declaring a page "tanking" or "winning", explicitly check (a) rank movement, (b) GSC click trajectory pre-vs-post AND YoY (seasonality matters in travel), (c) conversion/sales data if available, (d) ask Anna for qualitative context. When data sources disagree, lead with the disagreement, not a forced synthesis. Default suspicion: aggregate clicks looking flat or up may be branded traffic backfilling lost generic traffic — check the query mix.
