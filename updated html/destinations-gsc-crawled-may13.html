Anaheim, California in May

Fall in Anaheim transforms the region into a harvest wonderland where families can trade early Alberta snow for sunny days and world-class Halloween celebrations. At Disneyland Resort, the season brings the Main Street Pumpkin Festival and the Haunted Mansion Holiday makeover. Nearby, Knott’s Spooky Farm offers a daytime experience for younger children with trick-or-treating and seasonal shows. For a local tradition, the Anaheim Fall Festival and Halloween Parade features homemade floats and costumes through the downtown area. Beyond the theme parks, families can visit Discovery Cube Orange County for interactive exhibits like Pumpkin Science or head to the Honda Center as the Anaheim Ducks begin their hockey season in October. The autumn climate is particularly appealing for Albertans, with daytime temperatures that remain comfortably warm while evenings cool down enough for light layers. Visiting between mid-September and early November often provides the benefit of lower crowd levels before the winter holiday rush begins. This season allows for a more relaxed pace when exploring the Anaheim Packing District or the easy trails at Oak Canyon Nature Center. Choosing Anaheim for a fall family getaway ensures a perfect balance of festive magic and outdoor adventure without the intense summer heat. AMA Travel can help navigate the variety of seasonal events and complex ticket options to ensure every family member has a memorable experience. Their local expertise and travel support provide peace of mind, allowing you to focus on creating lasting traditions in the California sunshine.

Arizona in May

Fall in Arizona offers families a vibrant transition as the desert heat mellows, golden aspens transform the high country, and seasonal festivals bring a harvest atmosphere to the valley. In Flagstaff, known as the fall capital of the state, children can wander through the golden canopy of the Aspen Nature Loop or ride the Arizona Snowbowl Scenic Gondola for a bird's eye view of the autumn colours. Nearby Williams features the Grand Canyon Railway, which often hosts a festive Pumpkin Patch Train during October to delight younger travellers. The South Rim of Grand Canyon National Park is especially appealing this season with approximately 40 percent fewer crowds than summer, allowing families to explore the Junior Ranger program at a comfortable and relaxed pace. In the Phoenix and Scottsdale area, harvest celebrations such as the Pumpkin and Chili Party at Schnepf Farms offer exciting hayrides and elaborate corn mazes for children. For an educational twist, the OdySea Aquarium provides immersive marine experiences that complement the outdoor adventures found at places like MacDonald’s Ranch. The moderate autumn temperatures are ideal for hiking family-friendly paths in the Sonoran Desert. Packing layers is essential because while the valleys remain warm, the higher elevations often experience a crisp chill. Arizona in the fall provides a perfect balance of outdoor exploration and festive seasonal activities for every generation. AMA Travel can help you coordinate these diverse experiences to ensure your family holiday is both seamless and memorable.

Banff, Alberta in May

Fall in Banff transforms the Rocky Mountains into a golden playground for families, where the crisp alpine air and vibrant larch trees create a magical backdrop for outdoor exploration. Families can ride the Banff Gondola up Sulphur Mountain to enjoy the interactive Discovery Centre or take a scenic stroll along the summit boardwalk. For an accessible adventure, the steel catwalks of Johnston Canyon lead past dramatic waterfalls and rugged limestone cliffs. Wildlife enthusiasts may spot elk grazing near the townsite, especially during the autumn rutting season when activity peaks. Cultural experiences also shine with the Banff Mountain Film and Book Festival in late October, which offers engaging screenings suitable for all ages. This time of year is particularly appealing because the summer crowds have thinned, allowing for a more peaceful visit to iconic locations like Lake Louise. The weather remains mild enough for rewarding hikes up Tunnel Mountain or to the historic Lake Agnes Tea House, though be aware the tea house itself closes for the season in early October. Visiting during this shoulder season gives families more flexibility and a chance to experience the mountains at their most colourful. Banff in fall is an exceptional choice for multi-generational trips seeking both relaxation and gentle adventure. AMA Travel provides the local expertise and membership benefits needed to make every mountain getaway feel effortless.

Calgary, Alberta in May

Fall in Calgary transforms the city into a golden playground for families, where river valley parks glow with yellow larch and cottonwood trees while local farms celebrate the harvest season with corn mazes and pumpkin patches. The crisp autumn air is ideal for exploring the Calgary Farmyard, which features a massive corn maze and festive Harvest Lights after dark. For younger children, Butterfield Acres offers tractor-pulled wagon rides and a dedicated pumpkin fest perfect for making seasonal memories. As the temperatures cool, families can enjoy unique seasonal events such as Halloweekends at Calaway Park or Ghouls’ Night Out at Heritage Park, which offers a spooky but kid-friendly atmosphere. The Calgary Zoo also hosts ZooBOO, allowing little ones to trick-or-treat while visiting the animals. For indoor learning, the TELUS Spark Science Centre provides interactive exhibits and a massive dome theatre that keeps curious minds engaged when the weather turns chilly. This season is particularly appealing because the summer crowds have dispersed, leaving more room to explore iconic spots like the Central Library or Bowness Park. The vibrant foliage in Fish Creek Provincial Park offers a stunning backdrop for family walks and bird watching. Calgary in the fall provides a balanced mix of outdoor adventure and cozy indoor culture that appeals to all ages. Whether you are seeking harvest festivals or educational museum visits, AMA Travel can help you coordinate a detailed itinerary to ensure your family makes the most of the short but beautiful autumn season in southern Alberta.

Cancun in May

Fall in Cancun offers families a unique blend of vibrant cultural celebrations and quieter beaches, where the transition from tropical warmth to the mild air of November creates a comfortable setting for multi-generational exploration. One of the season's most captivating experiences is the Festival of Life and Death at Xcaret, where the park transforms with colourful altars and theatrical performances to honour the Day of the Dead. Younger children will enjoy the interactive nature of sea turtle releases frequently hosted by beachfront resorts during September and October. For a dose of history that is easy for small legs to navigate, the El Rey Ruins provide an accessible look at Mayan culture right within the Hotel Zone. Families seeking a day on the water can board a ferry to Isla Mujeres to explore the island via golf cart and swim in the shallow, calm waters of Playa Norte. This season is particularly appealing because the typical tourist crowds thin out, allowing families to enjoy popular attractions at a more relaxed pace. While the region remains warm, the drop in humidity by November makes outdoor activities like snorkelling at Xel-Ha or boarding a pirate dinner cruise much more pleasant for children. Choosing Cancun during this shoulder season offers an excellent balance of value and authentic Mexican atmosphere. AMA Travel ensures Alberta families are well supported with expert advice on weather insurance and specialized resort recommendations, making it easier to focus on creating lasting memories together in this Caribbean paradise.

Disneyland, California in May

Fall at Disneyland Resort transforms the theme parks into a whimsical autumn wonderland where families can enjoy mild California sunshine, festive Halloween transformations, and unique seasonal treats. The season is headlined by the Main Street Pumpkin Festival, where hundreds of hand-carved jack-o-lanterns decorate the iconic thoroughfare. Families with children of all ages can experience the Haunted Mansion Holiday, a seasonal overlay featuring characters from Nightmare Before Christmas, or head to Disney California Adventure Park for Plaza de la Familia. This vibrant celebration of Coco includes folk dancers and mariachi music, offering a culturally rich experience for every generation. For those seeking slightly more thrills, Guardians of the Galaxy Monsters After Dark provides a spooky evening adventure. Beyond the Disney gates, the Anaheim Halloween Parade and Fall Festival offers a community tradition that has entertained families since approximately 1924 with creative floats and costumes. This season is particularly appealing for families because the weather is comfortably warm without the intense heat of summer, and crowd levels are often more manageable. It provides a perfect escape for Albertans looking to trade early snow for pumpkin spice beignets and sunny outdoor adventures. Planning a multi-generational trip can be complex, and having the expertise of an AMA Travel counsellor ensures every detail is handled from park reservations to finding the best family suites. Disneyland in the fall offers a magical balance of spooky fun and heartwarming traditions that create lasting memories for travellers of all ages.

Dominican Republic in May

Autumn in the Dominican Republic offers a lush, tropical playground where families enjoy quieter beaches and the vibrant start of the professional baseball season. Catching a provincial game between October and December provides an authentic, high-energy experience for sports-loving families since baseball is the national pastime. For active children, Bavaro Adventure Park features Dino World and ziplining, while the natural cenote at Scape Park, known as Hoyo Azul, offers refreshing swimming in stunning turquoise waters. If visiting in late October, many all-inclusive resorts host elaborate Halloween celebrations, while the Puerto Plata Festival showcases local folk dancing and authentic food fairs. The fall season brings comfortable temperatures and warm ocean waters, ideal for extended afternoons at the beach. While brief tropical showers occur, they provide a welcome cooling effect on humid days. The diverse landscape provides a beautiful backdrop for exploration, including the shaded trails of the Indigenous Eyes Ecological Reserve. During this season, families benefit from lower tourist numbers at popular landmarks and more competitive rates for accommodations. The Dominican Republic remains a premier choice for multi-generational groups seeking a balance of adventure and poolside relaxation. Working with AMA Travel ensures families have expert support to navigate the varied regions of this Caribbean gem with absolute confidence and ease.

Edmonton, Alberta in May

Edmonton in the fall is a playground of golden River Valley trails and harvest festivals that turn the city into a vibrant autumn retreat for families. As the leaves change, the city’s vast urban parkland offers a stunning backdrop for outdoor exploration and seasonal celebrations. Families wander through the approximately 15-acre Edmonton Corn Maze or head north to Prairie Gardens and Adventure Farm for the Haunted Pumpkin Festival. For a unique view of the changing colours, the High Level Bridge Streetcar carries passengers high above the North Saskatchewan River. Nature lovers visit Elk Island National Park, where kids can spot bison against the autumn foliage. If the weather turns crisp, the Royal Alberta Museum features a wonderful interactive children’s gallery. Science enthusiasts will enjoy the TELUS World of Science, which offers hands-on exhibits like CuriousCITY. Sports fans can catch an Edmonton Elks football game or watch the Oilers return to the ice at Rogers Place. The fall season is ideal for families because the cooler temperatures and thinning summer crowds make exploring attractions much more comfortable. With lower tourist numbers, visiting popular spots like West Edmonton Mall or the many local festivals feels more personal and relaxed. Edmonton provides a perfect mix of outdoor harvest fun and world-class indoor attractions that keep every generation entertained. AMA Travel can help you plan the ultimate autumn getaway with member-exclusive rates on hotels and local attractions to ensure your family makes the most of the season.

Florida in May

Fall in Florida offers families a perfect balance of mild temperatures and festive celebrations, providing a comfortable environment for outdoor exploration without the peak humidity of summer. Orlando becomes a seasonal playground with events like Mickey’s Not-So-Scary Halloween Party at Magic Kingdom, where children can trick-or-treat through the park and enjoy specialized parades. At EPCOT, the International Food and Wine Festival includes kid-friendly scavenger hunts that keep younger travellers engaged while adults sample global flavours. Beyond the theme parks, families can visit Southern Hill Farms for sunflower picking and pumpkin patches or explore one of the largest corn mazes in the state at Great Scott Farms. For a unique wildlife encounter, the cooler autumn waters prompt manatees to migrate toward the natural springs, with official manatee season starting in mid-November at locations like Crystal River and Blue Spring. The Space Coast provides educational appeal at the Kennedy Space Center, which is typically less crowded during the autumn months. Nearby, the historic streets of St. Augustine offer spooky ghost tours and corn mazes that capture the essence of the season. This transition season is ideal for families because the dropping humidity makes long days at attractions much more manageable for all ages. With summer crowds vanished and pleasant evenings perfect for beach bonfires, parents can enjoy a more relaxed pace while children participate in seasonal harvest traditions. Florida in the autumn is a versatile choice for multi-generational groups seeking both adventure and relaxation. AMA Travel helps navigate these diverse seasonal experiences.

France in May

France in the fall transforms into a vibrant playground where families can enjoy golden harvest festivals and mild temperatures without the intense summer crowds. In Paris, the Jardin du Luxembourg and Tuileries Garden offer stunning autumn foliage while the Montmartre Grape Harvest Festival brings lively parades and food stalls in early October. Younger children will appreciate the vintage rides at Jardin d'Acclimatation, a whimsical amusement park located in the Bois de Boulogne. In the Loire Valley, the massive grounds of Château de Chambord provide a perfect setting for exploration and the pumpkin festival at Rivau Castle captures the spirit of the season. Families can also take a boat trip under Château de Chenonceau to see the fall colours reflected in the water. For an educational adventure, the prehistoric replica of Lascaux IV in the Dordogne fascinates all ages, especially when combined with a family canoe trip past medieval cliffs along the river. This transition season offers practical benefits for families, including shorter wait times at iconic landmarks and the festive atmosphere of Halloween at Disneyland Paris. The crisp air is ideal for strolling through local markets to sample seasonal roasted chestnuts and fresh apple tarts. France in autumn provides a rich, multi-generational experience that blends history with seasonal magic. Your AMA Travel counsellor can help curate a tailored itinerary that manages the logistics of regional travel, allowing your family to focus on creating lifelong memories together.

Greece in May

Fall in Greece offers families a chance to explore ancient wonders like the Acropolis under mild skies, where the summer heat has cooled to comfortable temperatures for young explorers and parents alike. This transition season is ideal for visiting Athens, where children can engage with history through mythology-themed tours of the Parthenon or enjoy the playgrounds at the Stavros Niarchos Foundation Cultural Centre. Just a short drive away, the Peloponnese region invites families to race in the original Olympic stadium at Ancient Olympia or explore the pedestrian streets of Nafplio. Autumn is also the season of harvest, providing unique hands-on experiences. Families can participate in olive picking at local groves or attend local festivals celebrating regional specialties like pistachios and chestnuts. On the island of Crete, the southernmost and largest of the Greek islands, the seawater often remains warm enough for swimming well into late October, making it a fantastic spot for a late-season beach day. Choosing fall means navigating fewer crowds at iconic landmarks and enjoying a more relaxed pace. The weather is generally pleasant, though evenings may feel crisp, perfect for sharing a warm koulouri from a street vendor in Monastiraki Square. This time of year transforms a standard history lesson into a vibrant, multi-generational adventure. Greece in the fall provides families with lasting memories of cultural festivals and scenic landscapes. Working with AMA Travel ensures your family’s complex itinerary is handled with local expertise and dedicated support for a worry-free Mediterranean escape.

Hawaii in May

Fall in Hawaii offers families a unique blend of warm ocean temperatures and cultural festivities like the Aloha Festivals, where floral parades and traditional performances bring the islands’ history to life without the peak summer crowds. In September and October, the water remains at its warmest, making it one of the best times for children to snorkel at Poipu Beach or explore the Maui Ocean Center’s interactive exhibits. Adventure-seeking families can visit the pumpkin patches at Aloun Farms on Oahu or Kula Country Farms on Maui, offering tropical autumn experiences with sweeping mountain backdrops. For an educational day, the Pearl Harbor National Memorial provides a moving history lesson with shorter wait times during this shoulder season. On the Big Island, the cooler autumn air makes exploring the steam vents and lava tubes at Hawaii Volcanoes National Park much more comfortable for younger hikers. This season is particularly appealing because the transition into the rainy season usually holds off until late October, and the lower humidity levels are pleasant for outdoor activities. While early November is typically quieter, it is wise to plan ahead for the late-month holiday rush. Families will appreciate the more relaxed pace at popular resorts and the increased availability at local restaurants. Whether you are seeking a multi-generational getaway or a school-break adventure, the islands provide a balanced pace of relaxation and exploration. AMA Travel specialists can help manage the complexities of island-hopping and booking family-friendly accommodations, ensuring your autumn retreat is perfectly suited to your needs.

Honolulu, Hawaii in May

Honolulu in the fall offers families a warm tropical escape where the crowds of summer have thinned, the ocean water is at its warmest, and unique cultural celebrations bring the island to life. This shoulder season is perfect for exploring the Bishop Museum where kids can witness a simulated volcano eruption at the Science Adventure Center or visiting the Pearl Harbor National Memorial to participate in the Junior Ranger program. Families visiting in September can enjoy the Aloha Festivals, featuring a colourful floral parade with traditional horseback riders. For a tropical twist on autumn traditions, Waimānalo Country Farms hosts a Fall Harvest Festival complete with pumpkin patches, sunflower fields, and cow train rides against a backdrop of mountain views. In October, the Honolulu Zoo offers guided evening tours that explore the more mysterious side of the animal kingdom. The weather remains pleasantly warm, typically around the mid-twenties in Celsius, which is ideal for hiking Diamond Head or snorkeling among green sea turtles at Turtle Canyon. Lower tourist numbers mean easier access to popular spots like Hanauma Bay or the calm, family friendly waters of Magic Island Lagoon. This season provides a balanced mix of educational discovery and relaxed beach time without the peak season rush. Honolulu in the fall is a fantastic choice for Alberta families looking to extend their summer memories. AMA Travel provides the local expertise and support needed to manage complex reservations and custom itineraries, ensuring your Hawaiian getaway is both smooth and memorable for every generation.

Iceland in May

Fall in Iceland offers families a vibrant landscape of rust-coloured lava fields and golden birch forests, providing a spectacular backdrop for educational adventures before the deep winter chill sets in. The Golden Circle remains a family favourite where children can witness the Strokkur geyser erupting approximately every few minutes and explore the historic Þingvellir National Park. Along the South Coast, the dramatic Seljalandsfoss allows visitors to walk behind the cascading water, while the nearby Skógafoss offers a misty spectacle perfect for photography. In Reykjavik, the Perlan Museum provides an interactive experience with its indoor ice cave and planetarium, making it an excellent refuge if the autumn weather becomes unpredictable. For a unique tradition, visiting in September allows families to witness Réttir, the annual sheep round-up where farmers gather flocks from the highlands. This season is appealing for Albertans used to crisp autumn air as temperatures remain relatively mild and summer crowds have dissipated. The return of darker nights marks the beginning of the Northern Lights season, offering a chance to see the aurora borealis without the extreme cold of mid-winter. Families also benefit from lower costs for car rentals and accommodation during this shoulder season. The combination of natural wonders and accessible cultural experiences makes Iceland an ideal autumn getaway for all ages. AMA Travel can help coordinate the complex logistics and family-friendly itineraries required for a seamless Nordic adventure.

Ireland in May

Ireland in autumn is a magical playground for families where ancient Halloween traditions blend with vibrant landscapes of crimson and gold. This transition season offers a perfect balance of mild temperatures and fewer crowds for exploring the Emerald Isle. Families can celebrate where Halloween began at the Púca Festival in County Meath or Europe’s largest Halloween festival in Derry, featuring parades and spectacles for all ages. For nature-loving families, Killarney National Park is a highlight during the red deer rut, while the Wicklow Mountains showcase stunning fall foliage. Children will enjoy a visit to Beyond the Trees Avondale to experience the treetop walk and its approximately 90-metre spiral slide. Another favourite is Birr Castle Demesne, which houses a science centre. The autumn atmosphere is ideal for families seeking authentic experiences without the summer rush. While the days grow shorter, the crisp air and occasional soft rain make the coastal drives and forest walks even more atmospheric. Hearty Irish stews and seafood chowders in welcoming village pubs offer a cozy refuge after a day of outdoor adventure. Ireland in the fall provides an educational and engaging journey for multi-generational travellers. Whether you are navigating the Wild Atlantic Way or exploring ancient ruins, the expertise provided by AMA Travel ensures your family itinerary is seamless. Their local knowledge helps families discover hidden gems and festive events that make an Irish autumn truly unforgettable.

Jamaica in May

Jamaica in the fall provides a peaceful island escape for families where the transition into the rainy season brings brief cooling showers and significantly smaller crowds at world famous attractions. Families can head to Ocho Rios to climb the natural limestone staircase of Dunn’s River Falls or experience the excitement of the Jamaican Bobsled at Mystic Mountain. For a more relaxed pace, drifting down the Martha Brae River on a bamboo raft offers a gentle way to see the lush tropical landscape. Visiting in October or November means enjoying National Heroes Day celebrations or taking advantage of Restaurant Week where local flavours become even more accessible. The calm, shallow waters of Doctor’s Cave Beach in Montego Bay remain perfect for younger children to swim safely during these months. This season is particularly appealing for families because off-season pricing often results in significant savings on accommodations and excursions. While the afternoons might see a quick tropical rainfall, these moments offer a refreshing break from the humidity before the sun returns. Avoiding the busy winter peak allows for a more intimate and authentic connection with the island's vibrant culture and friendly locals. Jamaica in the fall is an ideal choice for Alberta families looking to extend their summer memories before the northern winter begins. Navigating the variety of all-inclusive resorts and seasonal events is easier with the professional guidance and local expertise provided by AMA Travel specialists who ensure every family detail is handled.

Japan in May

Autumn in Japan offers families a vibrant landscape of crimson maples and golden ginkgo trees, paired with mild temperatures that make exploring bustling cities and peaceful parks comfortable for all ages. In Tokyo, Showa Kinen Park provides a massive outdoor playground where children can run through the famous Ginkgo Avenue or enjoy imaginative play areas like its bouncy domes and dragon-themed structures. Kyoto comes alive during the fall harvest, and a ride on the Sagano Scenic Railway offers breathtaking views of the river valley transformed by autumn colours before families visit the nearby monkey park. For a hands-on experience, Nara Park allows children to feed the tame deer amidst a backdrop of vibrant yellow leaves, creating an unforgettable multi-generational experience. Seasonal treats like roasted sweet potatoes and autumn fruit picking at local farms add a delicious layer to the journey. The transition from summer heat to crisp fall air is ideal for families who want to avoid the humidity while enjoying outdoor festivals and illuminated night gardens. Crowds are manageable, and child-friendly amenities like luggage forwarding and themed family suites make navigating the country seamless. Japan during the autumn months is a playground of colour and culture that leaves a lasting impression on young and old alike. Planning such a detailed trip is made easier with AMA Travel’s expertise, ensuring your family focuses on making memories while we handle the intricate logistics.

Jasper, Alberta in May

Fall in Jasper National Park is a golden season for families, offering crisp mountain air and vibrant yellow aspen forests without the bustling crowds of summer. Families can take a seven-minute flight on the Jasper SkyTram up Whistlers Mountain to see 360-degree views of the Athabasca Valley transitioning into autumn hues. For a quieter pace, the stroller-friendly walk across the wooden bridge to Pyramid Island provides a classic mountain backdrop framed by golden trees. October marks the start of Dark Sky season, where the Jasper Planetarium hosts interactive dome theatre shows and telescope tours perfect for curious young minds. This time of year also brings the unique sound of bull elk bugling throughout the townsite, providing a natural educational experience from a safe distance. Traveling during autumn allows families to enjoy a slower pace while exploring Jasper’s natural wonders. The mild daytime temperatures are ideal for hiking around Edith Lake or visiting the powerful Athabasca Falls. Kids will especially love seasonal treats like pumpkin pie fudge from the local Rock and Jade Shop or warm meals by the fireplace at Papa George’s. Since this is a quieter time for the park, parents often find more space at popular sites and a relaxed atmosphere in the town centre. Jasper in the fall offers an enriching mountain getaway that creates lasting memories for every generation. AMA Travel can help you navigate local tours to ensure your family’s autumn retreat is perfectly planned.
