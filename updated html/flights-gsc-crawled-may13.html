Flights FAQs

Still have more questions? Contact a travel expert.

AMA Travel is your best resource for Alberta flights. Our easy-to-use booking tool, competitive pricing, trusted support by Alberta-based travel experts, and ability to take care of all your travel needs at once combines for the easiest and most seamless trip experience. Also, booking with AMA Travel means always having someone with years of travel experience to talk to and help if any issues arise before, during and after your trip.

Since airlines control the prices of their seats, there is no foolproof method to always guarantee you’ve booked the cheapest available ticket. Travel experts suggest that being flexible on your travel dates (especially if the tickets for your preferred dates are higher than you have budgeted) is one good way to pay less for airfare. Also, flights with connections/multiple stops can sometimes (but not always) be less expensive than direct flights to that same destination. If you’re not set on a particular travel spot, you can also keep an eye out for AMA Travel last minute deals that offer significant savings when compared to regular priced travel packages.

One other suggestion made by our experts is to set your web browser to “incognito mode.” When searching for flights, search engines may pick up on the fact that you’re interested in going to a specific place at a specific time. In turn, they may keep showing you prices you’ve already seen, rather than lower ones that might be available to other people searching for the same flights.

Also, if you sign up for airline member programs (such as Air Canada’s Aeroplan or WestJet Rewards) you can receive member-exclusive discounts and points towards future purchases.

Generally as far in advance as possible, since it gets you the best availability. Also, if you book for future travel around holidays such as Black Friday, Boxing Day, etc., there can typically be additional savings.

There is no specific day of the week where flights are always going to be automatically less expensive than they are the other six days.

Unfortunately, due to the algorithmic and fluctuating nature of airline ticket prices there is no way to truly know or guarantee that you are purchasing a ticket at the lowest possible price. Some airlines do have monthly calendars showing dates with the lowest prices, but you need to be flexible with your travel plans.

While there’s no consistent magic hour where you’re guaranteed the lowest priced ticket, our travel agents suggest looking six months in advance. This is usually (but not always) when seats sell at their lowest price.
