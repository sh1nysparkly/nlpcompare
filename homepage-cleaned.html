South Korea in June

Summer in South Korea offers families a vibrant mix of high-tech indoor theme parks, refreshing mountain escapes, and coastal adventures where children can play in shallow waters while parents enjoy world-class seafood. In Seoul, families can escape the afternoon humidity at Lotte World, which features a massive indoor adventure area, or visit the COEX Aquarium for interactive marine exhibits. For a unique break from the heat, Gwangmyeong Cave provides a naturally cool underground experience that remains around 12 degrees year-round. Coastal Busan is another highlight, where the shallow shores of Haeundae Beach and the energetic Busan Sea Festival create a perfect atmosphere for kids. For active excitement, Caribbean Bay in Yongin serves as one of the country's premier water parks with dedicated zones for younger children and impressive wave pools. The extended daylight hours allow families to maximize their time, perhaps finishing a day of sightseeing with a picnic and splash pad visit at one of the Han River parks. While summer is humid, the abundance of air-conditioned transit, stroller-friendly malls, and refreshing treats like Patbingsu shaved ice make the season very comfortable for those travelling with children. This destination provides a safe and engaging environment for multi-generational travellers to create lasting memories together. Your AMA travel professional can help coordinate every detail of your South Korean journey, ensuring your family enjoys the best of this colourful culture with full peace of mind.

Jasper in June

Summer in Jasper National Park offers families a vast natural playground where long daylight hours provide ample time to explore turquoise lakes, mountain summits, and ancient glaciers. The town serves as a relaxed base for outdoor discovery, particularly at Lake Annette, where a paved loop and sandy beaches are ideal for strollers and picnic lunches. One of the park's most distinctive family experiences is the Columbia Icefield Adventure. Children can board a massive Ice Explorer to walk on the Athabasca Glacier and touch ice that is thousands of years old. For a bird's eye view, the Jasper SkyTram carries families up Whistlers Mountain to enjoy a boardwalk trail and panoramic vistas of the surrounding peaks. On the water, a classic Maligne Lake boat cruise leads to the famous Spirit Island, while a scenic float on the Athabasca River provides a gentle way for toddlers and parents to spot wildlife along the shoreline. The summer season is especially appealing for families because of the comfortable daytime temperatures and the opportunity to participate in the Jasper Planetarium's interactive programs. Visiting now also allows families to see forest regeneration first-hand, offering a unique educational perspective on the local ecosystem. Whether you are crossing the wooden footbridge at Pyramid Lake or enjoying a treat at Bear's Paw Bakery, Jasper in summer creates lasting multi-generational memories. For Alberta residents looking to navigate seasonal bookings or find the perfect cabin, AMA Travel provides the local expertise and support needed to ensure a seamless mountain getaway.

Palm Springs in June

Summer in Palm Springs transforms the desert into a playground of sun-drenched resort pools and refreshing mountain escapes, offering families a unique rhythm of morning adventures and cooling afternoon swims. To beat the heat, the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway is essential because it whisks visitors up to Mount San Jacinto where temperatures are approximately thirty degrees cooler than the valley floor. Families can explore pine-shaded trails or enjoy a meal with a view. For animal lovers, The Living Desert Zoo and Gardens in neighbouring Palm Desert is best visited at opening when the wildlife is most active and the splash pad provides a welcome break for children. The Children’s Discovery Museum of the Desert offers a fully air-conditioned retreat featuring dozens of interactive exhibits designed for experimentation. After the sun dips behind the San Jacinto Mountains, evening strolls through VillageFest on Thursday nights reveal local crafts and treats like the iconic date shake topped with a gourmet donut. This season is particularly appealing for families from Alberta looking for luxury resort experiences at exceptional value. Many local resorts feature elaborate water parks with lazy rivers and slides that keep children entertained throughout the day. Whether hiking through ancient canyons at sunrise or lounging poolside, Palm Springs in summer provides a memorable backdrop for multi-generational bonding. AMA Travel can help coordinate these complex family itineraries to ensure every detail of your desert retreat is handled with professional care.

Jamaica in June

Summer in Jamaica is a playground for families, where warm Caribbean waters and vibrant tropical festivals create an energetic atmosphere perfect for making memories together. In Ocho Rios, families can join a human chain to climb the terraced 600-foot Dunn’s River Falls or experience the Bobsled Jamaica ride at Mystic Mountain. For a gentler afternoon, the calm, clear waters of Doctor’s Cave Beach in Montego Bay provide an ideal setting for young swimmers. Summer is also the peak of festival season, highlighted by Reggae Sumfest in July, which includes a dedicated Family Fun Day featuring kid-friendly fairs and performances. The summer months are particularly appealing for families looking for value, as many luxury all-inclusive resorts offer significant seasonal discounts or kids stay free programs. While the weather is warm and tropical, occasional afternoon showers provide a refreshing break from the sun, and the extended daylight allows for leisurely bamboo rafting trips down the Martha Brae River. This season also brings local treats like fresh mangoes and Otaheite apples, adding a sweet taste of the island to your stay. With its mixture of outdoor adventure and relaxing beach life, Jamaica is a premier choice for an active family summer holiday. AMA Travel specialists are available to help manage every detail, from selecting the right resort with water parks to arranging private drivers for your daily excursions.

Victoria in June

Summer in Victoria transforms the garden city into a vibrant playground where families can enjoy the ocean breeze, extended daylight, and a wealth of outdoor activities. At the world-famous Butchart Gardens, children can ride the Rose Carousel while families enjoy nightly illuminations and Saturday fireworks during peak season. Downtown, the Inner Harbour comes alive with street performers and the delightful water ballet performed by the tiny harbour ferries. For a bit of history, the Royal BC Museum offers immersive exhibits like the life-sized woolly mammoth and an old-fashioned streetscape. Outdoor enthusiasts will appreciate Beacon Hill Park, which features over 200 acres of green space, a children’s farm with goat encounters, and playgrounds for all ages. Adventurous groups can head to the Malahat SkyWalk to enjoy views from a spiraling wooden walkway before descending on a massive slide. The mild Pacific climate is ideal for Alberta families looking to escape the dry heat, offering comfortable temperatures for exploring Fisherman's Wharf or whale watching on stable cruisers. While summer is peak season, the walkable nature of the city and the variety of hidden parks make it easy to find space to relax. Victoria in summer provides the perfect balance of educational heritage and high-energy fun for multi-generational groups. AMA Travel can help navigate the busy summer season by securing family-friendly accommodations and suggesting unique experiences like afternoon tea. Our experts ensure every detail is covered so you can focus on making memories along the British Columbia coast.

Anaheim in June

Summer in Anaheim brings the magic of Southern California to life for families, offering endless sunshine and extended daylight hours to explore world-famous theme parks and cooling water attractions. The iconic Disneyland Resort remains a centerpiece where kids can immerse themselves in the stories of Avengers Campus or enjoy classic thrills like the Matterhorn Bobsleds. During the warm summer months, families often head to Knott's Soak City to splash through Orange County's largest water park or enjoy the lively atmosphere of Knott's Summer Nights with its live music and unique treats. For a change of pace, catching a baseball game at Angel Stadium offers a classic American experience, especially during Family Sunday events that include post-game activities for children. The historic Anaheim Packing House provides a diverse and family-friendly dining environment, featuring a variety of local food stalls and an outdoor park area perfect for burning off extra energy. This season is widely considered a vibrant time to visit, as the city comes alive with nighttime spectaculars and outdoor concerts that make the most of the balmy California evenings. The peak summer season ensures that attractions are operating at full capacity, providing families with a wealth of entertainment options and special events like the nearby OC Fair. Planning a multi-generational trip during this busy period is made easier with the expertise of AMA Travel, ensuring your family finds the perfect balance of adventure and relaxation. Anaheim truly shines as a premier summer destination for families.
